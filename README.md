# Capri Launcher

**Capri Launcher** is a cyberpunk AI companion and customizable launcher inspired by Capri from the Capri Desktop project and Cyberpunk: Edgerunners.

Capri lives on the edge of your screen, talks to you (text + voice), has emotions, can change her appearance and voice, and helps you control your device.

## Features

- Capri appears on the **side of the screen** (draggable)
- Talk with Capri by **chat** or **voice**
- Change Capri's **avatar** with any PNG/image
- Change Capri's **voice** (including Fish Audio support)
- **Emotions system** (reacts when you treat her well or badly)
- Themes: Cyberpunk Neon, Cute/Kawaii, Liquid Glass, Minimal, Vaporwave, and custom
- Widgets support
- Open apps, control music, volume, brightness, websites, etc.
- Mini-games with Capri
- Help with programming, studying, reminders
- Bilingual (English + Spanish)
- Can be disabled per app or globally (Ghost Mode)

## How to Use Capri

1. Capri appears on the **left or right edge** of the screen.
2. You can drag her up and down.
3. **Tap** her to open the menu.
4. From the menu you can:
   - Chat with her
   - Talk by voice
   - Change her avatar
   - Change her voice
   - Change the theme
   - Open settings
5. Capri hides automatically when you open an app (and comes back when you return to home).
6. You can enable **Ghost Mode** to hide her completely.

## How to Customize Capri

### Change Avatar
- Open Capri menu → **Appearance** / **Avatar**
- Choose one of the official silhouettes or upload your own PNG

### Change Voice
- Open Capri menu → **Voice**
- Select default voice or connect **Fish Audio** (and other voice providers)

### Change Theme
- Open Capri menu → **Themes**
- Available styles:
  - Cyberpunk Neon
  - Cute / Kawaii
  - Liquid Glass
  - Minimal Dark / Light
  - Vaporwave
  - Custom (create your own)

### Change Language
- Capri uses your system language by default
- You can force English or Spanish in settings
